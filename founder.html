Mukesh Heerani

Founder & Creator of My Social

Mukesh Heerani is the founder and creator of My Social, a modern social networking platform designed to bring people together through meaningful connections, professional identity, and a secure digital experience.

Founder Profile

Mukesh Heerani is a Pakistani entrepreneur and technology enthusiast from Sindh, Pakistan. He is the son of Kewal Ram Heerani and is focused on building innovative digital platforms that create value for users.

Along with founding My Social, Mukesh Heerani is also the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Voxistar, working towards developing technology solutions and digital experiences.

Vision & Purpose

The vision behind My Social is to create a professional, secure, and user-focused social platform where people can connect, share their ideas, and build their digital presence.

Through technology and innovation, Mukesh Heerani aims to develop platforms that provide users with better online experiences while keeping privacy and security as priorities.
